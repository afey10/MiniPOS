At the counter

A checkout screen built for speed

Search or scan a product, build the order, and take payment — cash, card or bank transfer — without hunting through menus.

  • Product search and barcode scanning
  • Hold and recall orders
  • Discounts, notes and order types built in
Accountability

Voids and refunds stay controlled

Every order is logged with who rang it up, how it was paid, and its status — so a voided bill is always visible, not quietly gone.

  • Full order history with filters
  • Manager approval required to void a sale
  • Search by cashier, customer or order number
Stock

Know what's on the shelf, always

Stock adjusts automatically with every sale and purchase, with a full movement history so nothing disappears unexplained.

  • Live stock value and low-stock alerts
  • Manual stock adjustments with a reason logged
  • Per-product movement history
Reporting

  • Sales, products, categories, payments and expenses
  • Any date range, one click
