Features

Everything you need to run the counter

One system for sales, stock and staff — instead of a notebook, a calculator and a separate spreadsheet.

🧾

Point of sale

A fast, touch-friendly checkout screen. Add items, apply discounts, split payments, and print or send a digital receipt in seconds.

📦

Inventory management

Stock counts adjust automatically with every sale and purchase. Set low-stock thresholds so you restock before you're caught short.

🗂️

Product & category management

Organize your menu or catalog with categories, quick-add shortcuts, and pricing that's easy to update as things change.

🛒

Purchases & suppliers

Log purchase orders and supplier costs, print letterheaded POs, and keep your cost data in the same place as your sales data.

💵

Cash register tracking

Open and close registers per shift, log cash in/out, and reconcile at the end of the day without guesswork.

🔐

Role-based permissions

Cashier, manager and administrator roles each see only what's relevant. Sensitive actions — like voiding a bill — require manager approval.

📊

Live dashboard

Today's sales, order history and register status update in real time, so what you see on screen matches what's actually happening.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Staff accounts

Give every staff member their own login, track who did what, and remove access instantly when someone leaves.

📱

Installs like an app

Add it to a phone, tablet or computer home screen in two taps. No app store listing, no separate installer.

Purchasing

Purchases and suppliers, in the same system

Record what you bought, from whom, and what it cost — so your true margins are always visible next to your sales.

  • Purchase orders with supplier details
  • Stock received automatically updates inventory
  • Supplier list kept alongside your products
Expenses

Track running costs without a separate app

Rent, utilities, small purchases — log business expenses by category and see exactly how they stack up against sales.

  • Expense categories and date filters
  • Feeds straight into your profit reports
  • One place for everything the business spends
Built for real shifts

Designed around how small teams actually work

Every feature above exists because a real cashier, manager or owner needed it — not because it looked good on a spec sheet.
